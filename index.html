Introduction

Agent Trust refers here to the reliability, transparency, and verifiability of behavior and outcomes. Agent Safety focuses on preventing, detecting, and mitigating harmful behavior and risks. In our research, agent safety is one dimension of agent trust.

Our research covers trust and safety across the full single-agent process and the complete multi-agent communication lifecycle.
